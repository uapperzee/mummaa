Mumma, tamara mate ek letter 💌

Dear Mumma,

Aaje tamaro birthday che, ane aa divase hu tamne etlu kehva mangu chu ke tame mara jeevan ma ketla anmol cho. Tamaro prem mara mate hamesha ek surakshit jagya jevo rahyo che — jya hu game tevo hou, tamari pase mane samjan, himmat ane hunf male che.

Mara bachpan thi lai ne aaj sudhi, tame je rite mari sambhaal rakhi, mane margdarshan aapyu ane mara mate ghanu badhu karyu, ena mate hu dil thi aabhari chu. Tamara ghana prayatno ane balidaano shabdo ma samavi shakay eva nathi. Tame ghani vaar potana karta amari khushi ne pehla rakhi — aa prem hu hamesha yaad rakhish.

Mara mate tamari nani nani vaat pan khas che: tamaro prem bharyo awaaz, tamari chinta, tamari salah, ane ghar ne ghar banavti tamari hunf. Koi ek khas ghatna kehvani jarur nathi; mari ghani sundar yaado ma tame cho.

Tame mane prem karvo, sambhalvu ane mushkel samay ma pan aagal vadhvu shikhavyu. Hu je kai chu, ema tamaro moto haath che. Hu tamne hamesha garv anubhav karavva prayatna karish, tamari kadar karish ane tamari sathe prem ane aadar thi rahish — aa tamara dikrano vaado che.

Thank you Mumma, mara mate hamesha hajar rehva mate. Tamne duniya ni badhi khushi, aaram ane prem male evi mari dil thi dua che. Hu tamne shabdo karta pan vadhu prem karu chu.

Happy Birthday, mara favourite person. ❤️

Tamaro loving son,
Zeeshu
